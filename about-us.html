Systematic Cleaning Methodology

Our Proprietary Rotation Cleaning Process

At South Florida Cleaning Concepts, we utilize a meticulous rotation system to ensure your home is thoroughly cleaned on each visit.

Our initial deep clean focuses on the bathrooms and kitchen, complemented by a general cleaning of the rest of your home. On subsequent visits, we detail your rooms while maintaining the cleanliness of your kitchen and bathrooms. This ROTATION SYSTEM ensures a consistently clean home, ALL THE TIME.

Initial Visit: Bathrooms & Kitchen Intensive Deep Clean

Heavy descaling of tile, tubs, glass enclosures, stovetops, backsplash degreasing, microwave sanitization, plus complete vacuuming and dusting throughout the home.

Subsequent Visits: Detailed Living Area & Bedroom Care

Baseboard detailing, ceiling fans, blinds, upholstery vacuuming, door frame wipe-down, alongside continuous sanitary maintenance of kitchen and bathrooms.
