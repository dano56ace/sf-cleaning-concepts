Frequently Asked Questions

We aim to inform you about South Florida Cleaning Concepts. Find answers to common questions below.

What is the unique rotation system for cleaning used by South Florida Cleaning Concepts?

South Florida Cleaning Concepts uses a unique rotation system for cleaning that ensures every part of your home gets the attention it deserves. On the first visit, the focus is on an in-depth clean of your bathrooms and kitchen, while a general cleaning of the rest of your house is performed. On the subsequent visit, we detail your rooms and perform a general clean of the kitchen and bathrooms. This systematic approach ensures that your home is consistently clean.

How does South Florida Cleaning Concepts maintain the safety and security of my home?

The safety and security of your home and commercial facility is our absolute priority. All our cleaning professionals are thoroughly vetted, background-checked, and trained to respect your space. We also have procedures in place to securely manage keys and alarm systems, and we are fully insured and bonded for your peace of mind.

What experience does South Florida Cleaning Concepts bring with their 20 years in business?

With 20 years in the professional cleaning business, South Florida Cleaning Concepts brings a wealth of experience to every job. We are a family-owned and operated business, trusted by our clients to deliver high-quality, reliable cleaning services for residential and commercial spaces across Palm Beach and South Florida.

What range of cleaning services does South Florida Cleaning Concepts provide?

We provide deep cleaning for homes and offices, specialized commercial cleaning for restaurants and bars, medical facilities, schools, short-term rentals, post-construction cleaning, move in/out cleaning, home watching, and COVID-19 disinfection. Every service is customized to your exact schedule and specifications.

How does South Florida Cleaning Concepts ensure customer satisfaction?

Our mission is to ensure complete satisfaction with our services, backed by our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. We believe in our work so much that if we fail to make you happy, we do not expect you to pay. It’s that simple. If our services do not meet your expectations, let us know within 24 hours and we will re-clean the area at no extra cost.
