8-Point Scope

Our Comprehensive Post-Construction Cleaning Deliverables

We tackle every stage of post-construction residue, from rough debris clearing to micro-particle drywall dust extraction.

🔨 Debris Removal

We efficiently remove all leftover construction debris, packaging materials, and discarded hardware, leaving the property clean and tidy.

✨ Deep Cleaning

We provide an intensive deep cleaning of the entire property, ensuring every corner, ledge, baseboard, and crevice is spotless.

🌪️ Dust & Grime Elimination

We systematically extract fine airborne drywall dust and grime accumulated during construction or remodeling using HEPA filtration.

🪟 Window Detailing

Removing paint overspray, sticker adhesive, silicone, and mortar from glass panes, tracks, and frames for crystal-clear clarity.

🧼 Floor Scrubbing & Buffing

Cleaning, scrubbing, and buffing tile, hardwood, or polished concrete, removing construction residue and grout haze.

🧱 Wall & Trim Wipe-Down

Wiping walls, molding, doors, and safely removing contractor pencil marks, smudges, and dust accumulation.

🚿 Bath & Kitchen Sanitization

Full sanitization and polishing of brand-new fixtures, bathtubs, tile grout, and inside/outside cabinetry drawers.

💨 Ventilation & Register Cleaning

Cleaning HVAC returns and air registers to prevent post-construction dust from circulating through your newly built home or office.
