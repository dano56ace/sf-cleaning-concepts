9-Point Checklist

Our Detailed Move In/Out Cleaning Checklist

We leave no corner untouched, ensuring security deposit recovery and move-in perfection.

🧼 Deep Top-to-Bottom Clean

Complete top-to-bottom sanitization of the empty property, reaching baseboards, door frames, switch plates, and light fixtures.

🍳 Appliance & Cabinet Deep Clean

Degreasing ovens, scrubbing range hoods, wiping refrigerator shelves and freezer bins, and wiping inside and outside all cabinets.

🚿 Bathroom Disinfection

Eradicating soap scum, hard water scale, and mold from tubs, shower enclosures, tile grout, medicine cabinets, and toilets.

🪟 Windows, Tracks & Sills

Polishing interior windows, wiping dirty window sills, and vacuuming window slider tracks.

🧶 Carpet Steam Revitalization

Deep edge-to-edge vacuuming, spot stain treatment, and steam sanitization for refreshed carpeting.

🧱 Spotless Walls & Baseboards

Spot cleaning scuffs, fingerprints, and dirt marks along high-traffic hallways and door jambs.

✨ Floor Scrubbing & Polishing

Scrubbing and mopping tile, marble, or luxury vinyl plank, leaving floors gleaming and residue-free.

💨 Ceiling Fans, Blinds & Vents

Removing high cobwebs, wiping ceiling fan blades, detailed blind dusting, and wiping HVAC intake registers.

📦 Debris & Packing Removal

Disposing of remaining packing cartons, paper remnants, and discarded trash, leaving the space completely move-in ready.
