Creating Safe, Healthy & Sanitized Learning Spaces

South Florida Cleaning Concepts is proud to offer our esteemed School Cleaning Services, devoted to maintaining an optimal learning environment for students and staff. We understand the unique needs and demands of school cleanliness and are committed to providing exceptional, specialized cleaning services that prioritize the health and well-being of your educational community.

Targeted Areas

Specialized Educational Sanitation Protocols

We target high-germ educational surfaces using eco-friendly, non-toxic products safe for students of all ages.

📚 Desk & Classroom Sanitization

Wiping down all student desks, chairs, chalkboards/whiteboards, and door handles with child-safe disinfectants that curb virus spread.

🥪 Cafeteria & Table Treatment

Heavy-duty degreasing and food-safe sanitization of lunch tables, food trays, benches, and auto-scrubbing cafeteria floor tiles.

🏃 High-Traffic Hallway Care

Continuous floor sweeping, damp-mopping, and specialized stripping/waxing of corridor vinyl composition tile (VCT) for safety and sheen.

🚻 Restroom Disinfection

Hospital-grade sanitization of student and faculty restrooms, fixtures, partitions, mirrors, and replenishing hygiene consumables.

🏀 Gymnasium & Locker Rooms

Sanitizing athletic wrestling mats, bleachers, locker door latches, and shower stalls with antimicrobial broad-spectrum disinfectants.

💻 Library & Computer Lab Care

Anti-static delicate wipe-down of computer keyboards, mice, monitors, and dusting library shelves and study carrels.
