8-Point Inspection

Our Rigorous Home Watching Checklist

Each walkthrough inspects critical infrastructure and structural security points throughout your home.

🚪 Perimeter & Security Checks

Checking doors, windows, locks, hurricane shutters, and grounds for signs of intrusion, vandalism, or storm impact.

🌡️ Climate & Humidity Evaluation

Monitoring HVAC thermostats and indoor humidity levels to prevent rapid Florida tropical mold and moisture blooms.

🚰 Plumbing & Leak Assessments

Flushing toilets, running water taps, checking water heaters, and inspecting under-sink traps to eliminate stagnant water odors.

⚡ Appliance & Power Checks

Verifying refrigerator and freezer operation, ensuring GFI electrical circuits haven't tripped, and inspecting panel breakers.

🏊 Pool & Grounds Monitoring

Confirming pool pump water levels, checking sprinkler head operation, and overseeing landscaping contractor quality.

🪴 Indoor Plant Care

Watering indoor tropical house plants according to your exact schedules and care instructions.

📬 Mail & Parcel Collection

Collecting incoming postal deliveries, packages, newspapers, and flyers, forwarding urgent correspondence upon request.

📱 Digital Timestamped Reports

Delivering digital checklists accompanied by timestamped photographs directly to your email after every single property inspection.

Emergency Response When You Need It Most

In the event of severe tropical storm warnings, sudden AC breakdown, or water heater failures, our team provides immediate local coordination and dispatch with vetted trade specialists.
